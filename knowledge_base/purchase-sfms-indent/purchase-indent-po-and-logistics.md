# Purchase Indent, PO, Logistics, and Receipt Flow

The Purchase sidebar is a procurement workflow separate from Store FMS, though both deal with indents, vendors, POs, receiving, and payment.

## Purchase indent

Path: `/purchase/indent`

The Purchase indent API uses:

- `GET /vendors`
- `GET /purchase/materials`
- `POST /purchase/materials`
- `POST /purchase/indents`

Indent submission fields include:

- **Generated By**
- **Vendor**
- **Material**
- **Quantity**
- **Current Stock**
- **Notes**
- **Type of Indent** - Finished Goods, Raw Purchase, or Trading Purchase
- **Delivery Order No**

When a material is missing, the frontend can create a material with name, vendor, unit, and description before submitting the indent.

## Approval and vendor stages

After an indent is created, it moves through Purchase approval and vendor/procurement screens:

1. **Management Approvals** (`/purchase/managementApprovals`)
2. **Vendor Selection** (`/purchase/vendorSelection`)
3. **Purchase Order** (`/purchase/purchaseOrder`)
4. **Create PO** (`/purchase/createPO`)
5. **PO History** (`/purchase/poHistory`)

## Purchase order

The Purchase PO API supports:

- `GET /purchase/pos/liftable` - POs ready for lifting.
- `GET /purchase/pos` - all POs.
- `GET /purchase/indents/approved` - approved indents ready for PO creation.
- `POST /purchase/pos` - create a PO.
- `GET /purchase/pos/pending-document` - POs waiting for document details.
- `PATCH /purchase/pos/:id/document` - save PO number/copy/date/payment/transport details.

PO creation groups approved indent items by vendor/material and records quantity, rate, lead time to lift, total amount, advance amount, to-be-paid amount, when-to-be-paid, and notes.

The PO document step records PO No, PO copy URL, PO date, delivery date, transport type, payment terms, destination, terms, advance-to-be-paid flag, amount to be paid, and when-to-be-paid.

## Logistics, receiving, and quality

After PO creation:

1. **Lift Material** (`/purchase/liftMaterial`) - material lifting/follow-up.
2. **Receipt** (`/purchase/receipt`) - receipt check.
3. **Lab Testing** (`/purchase/labTesting`) - quality testing.
4. **Unload Management** (`/purchase/unloadManagement`) - unload approval/management.
5. **Lab Report** (`/purchase/labReport`) - lab result/reporting.
6. **Mismatch** (`/purchase/mismatch`) - quantity/quality/document mismatch handling.
7. **Purchaser Coord.** (`/purchase/purchaserCoordinate`) - purchaser coordination/follow-up.
8. **Debit Note** (`/purchase/debitNote`) - debit note branch for rejected/mismatched material.

## Accounts, audit, and return

Late-stage Purchase screens include:

- **Tally Entry** (`/purchase/tallyEntry`)
- **Advance Payment** (`/purchase/advancePayment`)
- **Accounts Audit** (`/purchase/accountsAudit`)
- **Purchase Return** (`/purchase/purchaseReturn`)

For payment requests that should go through the central Payment Workflow, use the Payment Workflow's Vendor Payment or Freight Payment helper pages, which can create payment requests from Purchase PO/logistics data.
